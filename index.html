MNotes Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

MNotes is a notes app for iPhone and iPad, made by Arnold Reddy. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

The short version

  • MNotes has no accounts, no servers, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Your notes, attachments, voice memos, tasks and settings are stored on your device.
  • A few features use Apple services or websites to work. They are listed below, and each one is used only when you use that feature.
  • The developer never receives your notes or any information about you.

What MNotes stores, and where

Everything you create in MNotes is stored on your device, in MNotes' own storage. That includes notes, pictures, drawings, scanned pages, attached files, voice memos and their transcripts, tasks, reminders, tags, folders and settings.

  • Backups. Automatic backups are kept on your device, in MNotes' folder in the Files app. When you choose Back Up Now, you pick where the backup file goes, such as iCloud Drive or another app. A file you save to iCloud Drive or share is then handled by that service.
  • Places. When you add a place to a note, its location is saved with the note. While the Nearby Notes screen is open, MNotes keeps your last 50 locations on your device to show the places you go most often. Nothing is uploaded.
  • The share extension. Text, links and pictures you share to MNotes from other apps are saved into your notes on your device.

Deleting MNotes removes everything it stored on your device. Backup files you saved elsewhere stay there until you delete them.

Features that use Apple services or the internet

These are used only when you use the feature:

  • Voice memo transcription. MNotes uses Apple's speech recognition. It runs on your device where possible. Otherwise the audio is sent to Apple to be transcribed, for example for longer recordings or when the language isn't available on your device. Apple's handling is covered by Apple's privacy policy.
  • Translation. Notes are translated with Apple's Translation framework, which runs on your device. Apple may download language files the first time you use a language.
  • Apple Intelligence (Organise, summaries and suggestions). On devices that support it, these use Apple's on-device model. Your notes are processed on your device.
  • Searching for a place. The text you search for is sent to Apple Maps to find matching places.
  • Links in notes. When you add a web link, MNotes loads that web page to show its title. The website sees an ordinary request from your device, as it would in a browser.
  • Opening directions hands the location to Apple Maps or Google Maps, whichever you choose.

Permissions

MNotes asks for permission only when a feature needs it. You can change these at any time in Settings › MNotes.

  • Camera: to scan documents and take photos for notes.
  • Photos: to add pictures from your library.
  • Microphone: to record voice memos.
  • Speech recognition: to transcribe voice memos.
  • Location (While Using): to add your location to a note and show nearby notes.
  • Location (Always): only if you turn on "Notify on arrival" for a note. iOS then tells MNotes when you arrive at that place, even when the app is closed. MNotes doesn't track your location in the background.
  • Calendars: to show your events and link them to notes. Events stay in your calendar app.
  • Notifications: for reminders, task due dates and arrival alerts. These are scheduled on your device.

Sharing

Information leaves MNotes only when you choose to send it: by sharing or exporting a note, saving a backup, or opening a link. Where it goes then is up to you and the app or service you choose.

Children

MNotes doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If MNotes changes what it does with your information, this policy will be updated and the date at the top changed.
