Common questions

Where are my notes stored? On your device. MNotes has no account and no server.

How do I back up my notes? In MNotes, go to Settings › Backup & Restore and tap Back Up Now. Save the file somewhere safe, such as iCloud Drive. MNotes also keeps weekly backups on your device.

How do I move my notes to a new iPhone? Make a backup on the old iPhone and save it to iCloud Drive. On the new iPhone, install MNotes, then go to Settings › Backup & Restore and restore that file.

Why didn’t “Notify on arrival” alert me? It needs location access set to “Always” and notifications turned on for MNotes. Check both in the Settings app › MNotes.
